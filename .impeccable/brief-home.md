# Surface brief: site shell and home

Scope: whole site (shell, home, inner pages). Visitor mode: Experience.
Audience and job: Dr. Tejash Patel and his office judging a speculative redesign; public readers in Gujarat on phones, in English or Gujarati.
Constraints: light only; facts from the live site only; bilingual from day one; GSAP + Lenis + Motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Charotar farmland surveyed from above; every section is a plot of land and the leader stands in his field. It refuses the leader-site default of hero banner, stat cards and carousels.

OWN-WORLD: Chalk-white ground ruled with fine navy furrow lines; whole sections are fields of paddy green or mustard yellow; navy ink type; one orange-red marker for pins, active states and actions. Anek type set wide and heavy, identical in Latin and Gujarati. Square corners, hairline rules, crop-row patterns, no cards with shadows.

STORY: He is the fourth generation of one farming family; the land explains the man. The visitor meets him, walks the family legacy, sees the offices he holds, the projects and awards, then contacts his office.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Chalk ground filled with living furrow lines that bend around the cursor. TEJASH upper left and PATEL lower right at about 17vw; his cut-out portrait stands centre-bottom in front of a tall mustard plot. Role line and one-sentence introduction bottom left with the primary action; tagline and scroll cue bottom right.

FORM: Cadastral field survey, candidate 7 of 7; seed key ea3e56fc. Signature interaction: the furrow field. Motion grammar: ploughing — rules draw, then content rises out of line masks and images open with a straight wipe; pages change with a strip wipe.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
