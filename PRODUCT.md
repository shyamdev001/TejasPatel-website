# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText), Lenis smooth scroll, Motion (Framer Motion) for page transitions and UI. User-specified libraries; framework delegated.

## Users

- The primary reader of this build is Dr. (H.C) Tejash B. Patel himself and his office: the site is a speculative redesign of https://www.tejashpatel.in/ meant to impress him.
- The public audience is farmers, cooperative members, bank customers, press, and government/cooperative-sector peers in Gujarat, mostly on mobile phones, reading in Gujarati or English.

## Product Purpose

The official personal site of Dr. (H.C) Tejash B. Patel, Chairman of The Kheda District Central Co-operative Bank (KDCC Bank) and a cooperative-sector farmer leader from Piplav, Anand district, Gujarat. It presents his legacy, positions, projects, initiatives, awards, press and gallery, and gives a way to contact his office. Success: he sees it and wants it to replace the current site.

## Positioning

Fourth generation of one farming family in the cooperative movement (Manibhai Patel → Bipinbhai Patel → Tejash and Sandeep Patel); founder-chairman of what the site calls the world's first solar cooperative; chairman or director across 13 farmer organisations. Tagline: "The Taste of Khedut".

## Content and constraints

- All facts, names, dates, awards, quotes and figures come from the live site; none may be invented.
- Bilingual: English and Gujarati, switchable, from day one.
- Images are pulled from the live site (static media and cms.tejashpatel.in).
- Light theme only (user decision). No dark theme.
- Must stay fast and usable on mid-range mobile phones.
- Routes to preserve: home, about, achievements, awards, projects (+10 project detail pages), initiatives, press, gallery, contact.

## Brand commitments

- Existing brand colours: navy `#1D2B77` and orange-red `#EA5136`; signature-style "Tejashpatel" logo.
- Tone: dignified public leader, rooted in farming; premium, not flashy for its own sake.
- User brief: Awwwards-level craft with rich typography, scroll-triggered animation, page transitions, micro-interactions and detailing.
